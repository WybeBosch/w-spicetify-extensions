# Random Playlist

Shuffle button next to the Your Library title. One click:

1. plays a random playlist from your library (your own, followed and Spotify playlists, inside folders too)
2. opens that playlist
3. expands its folders in the sidebar and jumps to it

It also pins the Your Library title in place, so the title and the button do not shift when you hover the header.

Uses `Spicetify.Platform.RootlistAPI`, `LibraryAPI.getContents` and `Spicetify.Player.playUri`.
